About NorthStar

NorthStar Academy is a small tutoring service for school students. We started with one idea: every student can do well in science and maths when someone explains it clearly and patiently.

Our teachers focus on building confidence along with marks, so students feel ready for exams and for what comes after school.

A good teacher gives students a fixed point to steer by, like the star we are named after.
